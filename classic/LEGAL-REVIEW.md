## Product facts reflected in the draft

- Local editing and storage vary by installation. Account vault, session and identity handling are implemented in [account storage and sessions](apps/web/src/accounts/server.ts), [Legitate OIDC](apps/web/src/accounts/legitate-oidc.ts), and [the local drive adapter](apps/web/src/app/api/local-drive/route.ts). The account session lasts up to seven days. Legitate's identity is linked through a verified subject, and the local link stores a hash. Legitate's own authentication runs on its service.
- [Saved-version storage](apps/web/src/accounts/storage-host.ts) can write encrypted snapshots to a chosen destination. [The peer relay](apps/web/src/peer-sync/relay.mjs) uses verified identity and connection metadata and holds bounded encrypted messages in memory. Reverse-proxy logs, hosting location and retention depend on the deployed infrastructure.
- [The Director provider](apps/web/src/generative/server/provider.ts) can submit selected content to an external generation provider. [OpenAI account integration](apps/web/src/ai/server/openai-codex-oauth.ts) and user-supplied provider keys require different notices and controls. Client material and metadata may leave the device when these functions are used. The draft makes no claim that every provider uses zero retention or excludes all training.
- [The app layout](apps/web/src/app/layout.tsx) loads Databuddy only when configured, including error tracking. [The configuration default](apps/web/src/env/web.ts) is off. Confirm which scripts and services load in the final production build before making statements about analytics, cookies or transfers.
- [The landing page](apps/web/src/components/landing/pocut-landing.tsx) shows a Free plan and US$5 Pro and links to the workspace. It does not establish a billing interval or enable checkout. [The repository license](../LICENSE) and any separate asset or model licenses are distinct from the hosted service terms.

## Release sign-off

1. **Operator and contact.** Confirm entity, legal and trading names, registration details if required, postal address, launch markets, and staffed private contact channels for support, privacy rights, accessibility and legal notices. Confirm controller or processor roles, designated privacy or accessibility contacts where applicable, and any required representative. Replace all references to the preview administrator and insert effective dates.
2. **Data inventory and rights.** Confirm each production recipient, hosting and relay locations, subprocessors, provider and model settings, contractual roles and transfer mechanisms. Approve the retention schedule for accounts, support, backups, security logs, telemetry and provider jobs. Test requests for access, correction, deletion, export, objections and complaints, including identity verification and copies outside POCUT. Approve a point-of-collection notice for account registration and contextual notices before enabling external transfers. Complete the production cookie and storage audit before enabling optional telemetry.
3. **Terms and checkout.** Confirm the actual price, billing interval, taxes, renewal, trial if any, cancellation path, refunds, mandatory disclosures and proof of agreement. Add a prominent online cancellation channel where required. Confirm age eligibility, liability, dispute terms and notices by market. Provide versioned terms at the point of agreement and preserve acceptance evidence. Avoid equating acceptance of the terms with consent to optional data processing.
4. **Accessibility.** Test the landing page, sign-in and account screens, timeline and editing controls, import and export, keyboard-only operation, screen readers, enlargement, contrast, focus and any checkout. Test representative web and desktop environments. Document specific limitations, practical alternatives, assessment date and method, contact and response process, local enforcement or escalation route where applicable, and any applicable physical-service arrangements. Publish a conformance status only after evidence supports it.
5. **Versions and localization.** Approve the English and Hebrew text together, then prepare and review legal translations for any additional launch languages and verify local pre-contract language rules. Keep the three routes reachable before sign-in, update the navigation when finalized, and remove the draft notices and search-indexing exclusions only at release sign-off.
