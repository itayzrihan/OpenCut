# Smart takes quality and timing

Status: **classic-only**, bridged through `OpenCutRuntime` and its generated MCP registry. The rewrite has no duplicate implementation. Browser code owns provider transport and audio decoding; Rust owns source evidence, ranking, cut boundaries, caption reflow and the atomic edit.

`timeline.classic.takes.prepare` reconciles the stored transcript with complete, valid word runs in source-linked captions inside the selected footage. This preserves editor timing corrections and recovers visible caption words missing from stale transcript metadata. Uncovered words and immutable source tracks remain archived. Ambiguous/trimmed caption clocks are not inferred. Word IDs always belong to the returned revision; previously cached plans cannot be reused against new evidence.

`timeline.classic.takes.edit` accepts optional `audioEvidence` for assembly: clip IDs and compact 10 ms clip-local timeline frames (`start`, `end`, `rms`, `peak`, `zeroCrossingRate`). The browser uses the same trim/rate/audio-offset mapping as playback and export. Rust reuses the conservative Smart silence analyzer, with full frame-coverage checks, transcript keep zones, 60 ms acoustic protection and bounded evidence. No decoded samples or media paths are persisted in the assembly.

Version 2 retains compact quiet ranges for reproducible alternative switching. It expands cuts outward into nearby verified quiet intervals, bounded by neighbouring omitted words, source clips and a 500 ms search distance. Without confident silence it keeps available 80/120 ms leading/trailing handles, limited to half the neighbouring gap, and reports `unverifiedBoundaries`. Quiet/noisy recordings and inaccurate ASR timestamps are not a guarantee of safe phonetic boundaries; the report must remain visible. Single-word stuttering is not inferred from energy or silently deleted.

Recommendations penalize repeated multiword phrases and short composite fragments, prefer complete continuous alternatives, and use boundary evidence as a ranking signal. Adjacent source-word parts are rendered continuously. Model prompts ask for complete sentence/clause alternatives and contextual review of repeats/false starts. All alternatives remain accessible; explicit editor selections are respected.

Caption words, video and synchronized tracks use the same integer-tick source-to-destination mapping. Version 2 captions preserve exact word clocks, start a new cue at every source edit, and carry presentation from the corresponding source location. Old cue-local trims/animations are not transplanted onto unrelated new sentences. Source captions and all alternatives remain in the undoable archive. Version 1 assemblies remain readable/selectable with their original mapping; they are not silently migrated.

The task checkpoint key is versioned to v2. Decoding can be cancelled; project/account/revision changes reject apply. Quality counts are returned by Smart takes status and persisted in `scene.takeAssembly.quality`. Compact per-clip audio diagnostics retain frame coverage, quiet-range counts and the analyzer's safety-hold reason so missing coverage can be distinguished from noisy recordings. An assembled project with review items gets a review notification rather than an unconditional clean-result message.

Regression coverage includes registry edits/readback, exact caption clocks after reorder and retime, late final words, cue boundaries, audible tails after ASR endpoints, continuous-part coalescing, manual alternative selection, audio-gap persistence/reopen, original archive preservation, dry-run, cancellation, optimistic conflicts and undo/redo.
